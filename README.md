# Fleet Operations Performance & Expense Control

**Operations Management | KPI Reporting | Process Improvement | Cost Control**

This portfolio case study demonstrates how I approached an operational visibility and expense-control challenge while working in logistics. The public materials use **sanitised sample data** and do not contain confidential company, customer, driver or financial information.

## Business Context

Coordinating a small logistics fleet requires more than knowing whether vehicles are moving. Reliable operations depend on visibility across trips, vehicle utilisation, operating costs, delivery issues and supporting expense records.

In my logistics work, I supported operations across a **5-vehicle fleet** and introduced a more structured expense-tracking process to improve visibility and reduce avoidable disputes around operational spending.

## The Operational Challenge

The workflow needed a clearer way to answer questions such as:

- Which vehicles were active and how frequently were they being used?
- What operating expenses were being incurred by vehicle and trip?
- Were expense claims supported by the required information?
- Where were delays, exceptions or recurring issues occurring?
- How could management get a faster view of operational performance?

Without structured tracking, routine operational questions could require manual follow-up and make reconciliation more difficult.

## My Approach

### 1. Standardise operational data

Defined a consistent structure for recording trip activity, vehicle information, expense categories, amounts, supporting documentation and operational status.

### 2. Create expense visibility

Introduced a structured expense-tracking process so costs could be associated with the relevant vehicle or operational activity and reviewed more consistently.

### 3. Track exceptions

Separated normal activity from issues requiring follow-up, making it easier to identify missing documentation, disputed expenses, delays and other operational exceptions.

### 4. Monitor performance

Used recurring operational information to support reporting and identify patterns rather than treating every issue as an isolated event.

### 5. Improve accountability

Created clearer records for review and reconciliation, reducing ambiguity around operational expenses and improving follow-through.

## Outcome

The improved expense-control process contributed to an approximately **90% reduction in expense disputes**, while providing stronger visibility into fleet-related operational activity.
