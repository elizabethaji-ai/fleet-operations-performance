# Expense Control Workflow

## Objective

Create a consistent audit trail for fleet-related operating expenses while reducing time spent resolving unclear or unsupported claims.

## Workflow

### 1. Expense Capture
Record the trip ID, vehicle, date, expense category, amount and business purpose as close to the activity as possible.

### 2. Evidence Check
Confirm that required supporting documentation is attached or available. Missing evidence is flagged rather than silently passed forward.

### 3. Validation
Check the expense against the relevant trip/activity and review unusual amounts, duplicate-looking entries or mismatches.

### 4. Status Assignment
Use simple statuses such as:

- **Approved** — information and evidence are complete.
- **Review** — clarification or verification is required.
- **Rejected/Returned** — the expense does not meet requirements and must be corrected.

### 5. Exception Follow-up
Assign unresolved items for follow-up and document the reason, owner and next action.

### 6. Reconciliation
Review approved records against the period's operating activity and resolve remaining exceptions before reporting.

### 7. Trend Review
Analyse recurring causes of disputes or missing information. Update guidance or controls where a pattern is identified.

## Control Principles

- One expense should be traceable to a clear operational activity.
- Exceptions should have an owner and next action.
- Supporting evidence should be checked consistently.
- Repeated problems should lead to process changes, not repeated manual fixes.

## Improvement Logic

The workflow reduces ambiguity by making the expected information explicit at the beginning of the process. This shifts effort from retrospective dispute resolution toward prevention and exception-based management.
